# Ranked Rumble derivatives must contribute to battle strategy

## Context

The public bot catalog supports learning and licensed reuse. Separate ranked entries for cosmetic copies can fill the competition with essentially the same bot. License permission to reuse code and eligibility for a separate ranking entry serve different purposes.

## Decision

Moderators may reject a near-duplicate of an existing catalog bot when its differences are primarily cosmetic and make no substantial contribution to battle strategy. Licensed derivatives with a substantial strategic contribution remain eligible under this rule. Similar behavior alone is insufficient evidence of duplication; independently written bots can use the same strategy.

Moderators compare the relevant source and strategy, consider the author's explanation, and record the compared entry, evidence and reasoning. The author may seek reconsideration by a moderator without a conflict of interest. No numerical similarity threshold or automatic plagiarism verdict is introduced. Existing automatic checks do not establish compliance with this human rule, and reported cases can be reviewed after automatic acceptance under the existing dispute procedure. Repeated member identities within one TwinDuel team remain valid.

The [catalog submission design](../design/rumble/bot-submission.md#distinct-ranked-entries) describes how the rule fits the existing moderation path.
