# Robocoders can join Rumble before its public announcement

**Who:** Bot authors and battle contributors in the Robocode community, including GitHub users outside the maintainer organization, and the maintainer preparing Rumble's announcement.

**What:** A documented, reproducible path to submit bot source, contribute ranked battles, and verify that accepted results reach the rankings, with consistent guidance across the public repositories and websites.

**Why:** The announcement should invite people into a competition they can actually join without undocumented maintainer privileges or personal help. This advances Tank Royale's existing purpose of learning programming through competition.

The maintainer requested the pre-announcement audit and directed that its findings become the next campaign. The [readiness audit](../analysis/AN-014-rumble-announcement-readiness.md) distinguishes observed documentation gaps from outsider-path risks that still need reproduction. This goal does not prescribe a replacement authentication mechanism.
