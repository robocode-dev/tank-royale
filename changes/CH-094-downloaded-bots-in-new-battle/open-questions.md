## Is the folder added by itself, or only when the person ticks a box?

Decided by the maintainer: by itself. The recommendation was: by itself, with one line on the result screen saying that the folder was added and where to switch it off. The plan and the mockups promise it, and the person never has to visit the settings to practise. The alternative is a ticked box "Use these bots in New Battle" on the result screen.

## Does Bot Info show only the license, or also its copy-permission level and a team's members?

The maintainer asked for a field in Bot Info that shows which license applies, and it is part of this change. The plan item also asks for a copy-permission level beside it (free to copy, copy with conditions, do not copy) and a team's members. Decided by the maintainer: only the license for now, as a link to its official page where one exists. The recommendation was: the license as written now, and the level and the team's members in a change of their own. The level needs its own reading of licenses beyond the four the Rumble allows, and a license field alone already answers the question of which license applies.

## Does the group remember whether it was open?

Decided by the maintainer: yes, closed each time New Battle opens. The recommendation was: no. The group is collapsed every time New Battle opens, so the person's own bots are always what they see first. Remembering it is a small addition later if it turns out to be missed.
