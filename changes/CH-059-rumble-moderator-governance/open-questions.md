Verify with an App installation token that the active membership lookup succeeds while an absent or pending member cannot approve. A second qualified volunteer can then be added by a team maintainer or organization owner after the published criteria and example review are applied. The bot repository change must be integrated before publishing the Tank Royale guide's volunteer-form link.
